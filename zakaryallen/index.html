About Zakary Allen

  • 2030 QB
  • Prescott, AZ
  • 3.8 GPA
  • Team captain
  • All-Star (Florida)

I am a 2030 quarterback from Prescott, Arizona. I take pride in being a versatile player who can impact the game in multiple ways, whether it’s leading an offense, making plays in space, or doing the small details that help the team win. I bring a competitive mindset and strong football IQ to everything I do.

Leadership is a big part of who I am as a player. I have competed in All-Star events, including playing in Florida, and I have also been selected as a team captain for the tackle football teams I’ve played for in Prescott. I value accountability, coachability, and being someone my teammates and coaches can rely on. I take feedback seriously and am committed to improving every aspect of my game.

Off the field, I take academics seriously and currently hold a 3.8 GPA. I understand the importance of discipline, time management, and character, and I try to represent myself, my family, and my program the right way. My goal is to continue developing physically, mentally, and technically so I can compete at the highest level possible.
